O namaAbout us

Ancora Nuova

Ancora Nuova d.o.o. Beograd se bavi uvozom, preradom i distribucijom smrznute i sveže ribe i plodova mora, kao i proizvodnjom ribljih delikatesa. Jedna smo od vodećih firmi na srpskom tržištu u ovoj oblasti.Ancora Nuova d.o.o. Belgrade imports, processes and distributes frozen and fresh fish and seafood, and produces fish delicacies. We are one of the leading companies in this field on the Serbian market.

Da bismo sačuvali najbolji mogući kvalitet, svežu ribu iz Italije, Grčke i Norveške dovozimo svake nedelje. Smrznuta riba stiže u kontejnerima-zamrzivačima iz celog sveta. Naše hladnjače primaju 600 tona smrznute i 50 tona sveže ribe i opremljene su najsavremenijom tehnologijom.To keep quality as high as possible, we bring in fresh fish from Italy, Greece and Norway every week. Frozen fish arrives in freezer containers from all over the world. Our cold stores hold 600 tonnes of frozen and 50 tonnes of fresh fish and run on up-to-date equipment.

U sopstvenom pogonu dimimo ribu i proizvodimo riblje specijalitete i plodove mora. Naš strateški proizvod je dimljeni losos, proizveden klasičnim postupkom suvog soljenja, koji mu daje najviši kvalitet. Proizvodimo ga i za privatne robne marke Ledo, Delhaize, Konzum i Mercator.In our own plant we smoke fish and produce fish specialities and seafood. Our flagship product is smoked salmon made the classic way, dry-salted, which gives it the highest quality. We also produce it under private label for Ledo, Delhaize, Konzum and Mercator.

Skladištenje i prerada su pod stalnim nadzorom naših stručnjaka, u skladu sa evropskim zdravstvenim i ekološkim standardima u prehrambenoj industriji, od izbora sirovine do gotovog proizvoda. Imamo HACCP i IFS Food sertifikat (sertifikaciono telo TÜV SÜD Czech).Storage and processing are under constant supervision by our specialists, in line with European health and environmental standards for the food industry, from the choice of raw material to the finished product. We hold HACCP and IFS Food certification (certification body TÜV SÜD Czech).

Savremeni kamioni hladnjače, pod stalnom kontrolom, dovoze ribu u ribarnice i supermarkete širom zemlje i u naše sopstvene fish barove i ribarnice. Redovni smo dobavljač velikih trgovinskih sistema kao što su Delhaize, Tempo, Univer i Aroma, snabdevamo veliki broj restorana i prisutni smo na tržištima bivše Jugoslavije.Modern, constantly monitored refrigerated trucks deliver fish to fishmongers and supermarkets across the country and to our own fish bars and fish shops. We are a regular supplier to major retail groups such as Delhaize, Tempo, Univer and Aroma, supply many restaurants, and sell across the former Yugoslavia.

Kvalitet, cena, sigurnost i brzina isporuke su osnova našeg poslovanja. Cilj nam je da postanemo regionalni lider u preradi ribe.Quality, price, safety and speed of delivery are the basis of our business. Our goal is to become the regional leader in fish processing.
